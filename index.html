Empowerment Guide

Safety Resources & Legal Rights

Knowledge is personal strength. Explore essential everyday safety strategies, legal provisions, and cyber defense tips curated for awareness.

Commute Safety

Safe Night Commute Strategies

When booking cabs or using public transport late at night, verify license plates before boarding, share your live ride status with a trusted contact, and prefer well-lit main routes.

  • Check child-lock status before closing vehicle doors.
  • Pre-plan your transit route and keep your phone charged above 30%.
Cyber Protection

Digital Stalking & Privacy Shield

Protect yourself from online stalking and harassment. Regularly audit social media location-tagging, review app background permissions, and never share home addresses publicly.

  • Turn off automatic metadata (EXIF) sharing on photos.
  • Report cyber harassment directly to national portal cybercrime.gov.in or 1930.
Constitutional & Legal

Right to "Zero FIR" & Legal Aid

A woman can file a Zero FIR at any police station regardless of jurisdiction if an emergency occurs. The police cannot refuse registration on territorial grounds.

  • Under Section 160 CrPC, women cannot be summoned to police stations for questioning at night.
  • Free legal services are guaranteed under the Legal Services Authorities Act.
Situational Awareness

Intuition & Boundary Assertion

Trust your instincts. If a person or situation feels uncomfortable or unsafe, exit immediately without worrying about politeness. Your personal safety always precedes social courtesy.

  • Keep hands free and head up when walking in unfamiliar areas.
  • A firm, loud "NO" or "BACK OFF" attracts bystander attention and discourages offenders.
Travel Preparedness

Everyday Commute Safety Checklist

Simple habits ensure peace of mind during daily travel to college, coaching classes, or workplaces.

  • Carry a charged portable power bank and emergency cash.
  • Set up quick-dial or double-tap emergency shortcuts on your smartphone lock screen.
Workplace Protection

POSH Act (Workplace Protection)

The Protection of Women from Sexual Harassment at Workplace (POSH) Act mandates every employer with 10+ staff to establish an Internal Complaints Committee (ICC).

  • Covers physical, verbal, and electronic forms of workplace misconduct.
  • Complainant identity is strictly safeguarded under non-disclosure clauses.
